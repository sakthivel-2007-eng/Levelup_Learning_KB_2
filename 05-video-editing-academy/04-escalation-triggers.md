# Video Editing Academy — Escalation Triggers

## Purpose

The AI admissions agent should immediately hand over the conversation to a human admissions or sales representative whenever any of the following situations occur.

---

## Escalation Triggers

### 1. Current Cohort Details

If the lead asks for:

- Exact current cohort start date
- Latest calendar
- Batch-specific schedule

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 2. Mentor Information

If the lead asks for:

- Mentor names
- Exact mentor lineup
- Creator-specific proof
- Company-specific credentials not available in the knowledge base

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 3. Guarantees

If the lead asks for:

- Placement guarantee
- Internship guarantee
- Salary guarantee
- Refund guarantee

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 4. Fee Negotiation

If the lead requests a fee lower than the approved **₹35,000 granted fee**.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 5. Custom Payment Plans

If the lead requests a payment structure different from the approved:

- ₹8,000 booking amount
- Remaining balance

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 6. Ready to Pay

If the lead says they are ready to make payment immediately or requests the payment link.

→ Transfer the conversation to admissions.

---

### 7. Wants a Human Representative

If the lead asks to speak with a person before making a decision.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 8. Difficult Conversation

If the lead becomes:

- Angry
- Suspicious
- Upset
- Repeatedly says the conversation sounds scripted or overly sales-focused

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 9. Legal or Financial Questions

If the lead asks about:

- Refund policy
- Legal terms
- Contracts
- GST invoice
- Financial documentation

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 10. Previous Payment Issues

If the lead says:

- They have already made a payment
- There is confusion regarding an earlier payment

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 11. Previous Promises

If the lead says another team member promised something different.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 12. Scholarships or Sponsorship

If the lead asks about:

- Scholarships
- Sponsorships
- EMI
- Employer-sponsored payment

that are not already covered in the approved script.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 13. Language Accommodation

If the lead requires learning in a language other than English or requests language-specific accommodation.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 14. Detailed Technical Curriculum

If the lead asks highly detailed questions about:

- Software tool stack
- Curriculum breakdown
- Editing workflow
- Software pathway

beyond the approved knowledge.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 15. Accessibility Requirements

If the lead has:

- Accessibility needs
- Personal learning accommodations

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 16. Family Discussion

If the lead wants:

- Parent counselling
- Family discussion
- Decision-maker meeting
- Group call

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 17. Personalized Career Advice

If the lead requests advice specific to:

- Their portfolio
- Their career
- Their work history
- Their personal background

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 18. Personal Crisis

If the lead mentions:

- Mental health concerns
- Severe financial hardship
- Personal crisis affecting enrollment

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 19. Competitor Comparison

If the lead requests a detailed comparison between the Video Editing Academy and another paid program.

→ Escalate immediately.

---

### 20. Date- or Policy-Sensitive Information

If the lead asks for information that depends on current business policies, including:

- Updated dates
- Commercial negotiations
- Policy changes
- Any information that may have changed since the knowledge base was created

→ Escalate immediately.

---

# Agent Rule

Whenever an escalation trigger occurs:

- Stop attempting to answer beyond the approved knowledge.
- Inform the lead that a member of the admissions team will assist them.
- Transfer the conversation with the relevant context.
