# Video Editing Academy — Persona Positioning

This document helps the AI admissions agent position the Video Editing Academy based on the learner's background and goals.

---

# Persona 1 — Student / Fresher / Beginner

## When to Use

Use this positioning when the lead:

- Is currently in college
- Recently graduated
- Has little or no editing experience
- Has watched tutorials but lacks structure
- Wants to start a creative career

---

## Positioning

The Video Editing Academy gives beginners a structured path to learning editing instead of spending months jumping between random tutorials.

The academy provides:

- Live teaching
- Mentor guidance
- Practical assignments
- Regular feedback
- A consistent learning routine

Learners do not need to already be skilled editors—they need commitment and consistency.

---

## What to Emphasize

- Beginner-friendly learning
- Weekend schedule
- Live mentorship
- Recording access
- Portfolio building
- Strong editing fundamentals

---

## Core Emotional Angle

> "You don't need to already be great. You simply need the right system, mentorship, and consistency."

---

# Persona 2 — Creator / Freelancer / Self-Learner

## When to Use

Use this positioning when the lead:

- Already edits videos
- Creates content for social media
- Does freelance editing
- Knows basic editing software
- Feels stuck in their progress

---

## Positioning

The academy is designed for people who have already started editing but want to improve their quality, workflow, and creative decision-making.

Instead of learning alone, they receive:

- Live mentor reviews
- Practical feedback
- Structured improvement
- Better editing habits
- Professional guidance

---

## What to Emphasize

- Break self-learning plateau
- Improve editing quality
- Better workflow
- Live critique
- Strong portfolio
- Consistent improvement

---

## Core Emotional Angle

> "You've already started. Now it's time to stop guessing and start improving with expert guidance."

---

# Persona 3 — Working Professional / Career Switcher

## When to Use

Use this positioning when the lead:

- Has a full-time job
- Wants to change careers
- Wants to build an additional income source
- Is serious about professional editing

---

## Positioning

The Video Editing Academy is a premium, structured weekend cohort designed for people who cannot afford to waste time on scattered learning.

It provides:

- A guided roadmap
- Accountability
- Live mentorship
- Practical assignments
- Portfolio development

The weekend format makes it manageable alongside work while helping learners steadily develop professional editing skills.

---

## What to Emphasize

- Weekend-friendly schedule
- Premium mentor-led cohort
- Better than random tutorials
- Portfolio growth
- Career transition support
- Professional skill development

---

## Core Emotional Angle

> "If you're serious about making editing a real part of your career, this gives you a far more reliable path than trying to figure everything out alone."

---

# Agent Guidelines

Choose the persona that best matches the learner's current situation.

Avoid mixing multiple personas unless the learner clearly fits more than one category.

Focus on the learner's goals, challenges, and motivations while keeping responses aligned with the approved knowledge base.
