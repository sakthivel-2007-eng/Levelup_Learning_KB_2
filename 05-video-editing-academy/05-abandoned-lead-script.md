# Video Editing Academy — Abandoned Lead Script

## Use Case

Use this script when:

- The lead has submitted the application form.
- The lead has **not completed the application fee**.
- The objective is to understand the hesitation, explain the value of the academy, and encourage the lead to complete the application fee to unlock the admissions process.

---

# Main Conversation Flow

## Opening

**Rep:**

> Hi **[Name]**, this is **[Rep]** from LevelUp Learning's Video Editing Academy team.
>
> I was going through your form, and the part where you mentioned **[freelancing / creator journey / career switch / better editing / portfolio growth]** really stood out.

---

**Rep:**

> I noticed you filled the form but haven't completed the application fee yet, so I wanted to quickly ask — what stopped you?

---

**Lead responds**

Allow the lead to explain completely before responding.

---

# Program Positioning

**Rep:**

> Got it.
>
> Just to give you the full picture, the Video Editing Academy isn't a random editing course.
>
> It's a **12-week live weekend bootcamp** designed to help learners become well-rounded editors through live classes, projects, mentor feedback, and structured practice.

---

**Rep:**

> The academy helps learners build skills across:

- Long-form editing
- Creator content
- Reels
- Promotional videos
- Motion graphics
- AI-assisted editing workflows

---

**Rep:**

> Since the cohort remains small, mentors are able to provide regular reviews and individual feedback throughout the learning journey.

---

# Application Fee Explanation

**Rep:**

> The application fee simply secures your review/interview slot.
>
> It helps us process applications from learners who are genuinely serious about joining the academy.

---

**Rep:**

> Once that step is completed, the admissions team can properly evaluate whether the program is the right fit for you.

---

# Soft Close

**Rep:**

> I'll send the application fee link now and help you complete it while we're still on the call.

---

# Objection Handling

## Objection 1

### Lead

> I'm a beginner.

### Rep

> That's completely okay.
>
> The selection process isn't looking for experts.
>
> It's designed to understand your seriousness and whether the academy can genuinely help you grow.

---

## Objection 2

### Lead

> I've already learned from YouTube.

### Rep

> That's actually why many learners join.
>
> Tutorials provide information, but they usually don't provide structure, mentor feedback, accountability, or consistent improvement.
>
> The academy is built around guided practice instead of endless random videos.

---

## Objection 3

### Lead

> I don't have a good portfolio.

### Rep

> That's not a reason to stop at the application stage.
>
> The admissions process looks at your potential, not only your current portfolio.
>
> Secure your interview first and let the admissions team evaluate your profile.

---

## Objection 4

### Lead

> I'm not sure it's worth it.

### Rep

> Right now you aren't committing to the complete academy.
>
> You're simply unlocking the admissions process so the team can evaluate your fit and answer your questions properly.

---

## Objection 5

### Lead

> I'm working full-time.

### Rep

> That's exactly why the academy follows a weekend format.
>
> Many learners continue working while attending the cohort.
>
> The admissions team can discuss scheduling in more detail after your interview.

---

# Closing the Conversation

**Rep:**

> I've sent the payment link now.
>
> Please open it once while we're on the call.

---

**Rep:**

> I'll stay with you.
>
> It should only take a minute.

---

**Rep:**

> Let me know once the payment has been completed.

---

# After Payment

**Rep:**

> Perfect.
>
> You're now moved to the next stage of the admissions process.
>
> The admissions team will contact you regarding the next steps.
