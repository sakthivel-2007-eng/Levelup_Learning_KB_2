# Escalation Triggers

The voice agent should stop pushing and transfer to a human admissions counselor when any of the following happens:

1. The caller asks for the exact current cohort start date.

2. The caller asks for mentor names or the current mentor lineup.

3. The caller asks for EMI details or a custom payment exception not loaded in the system.

4. The caller asks about refund policy or cancellation terms.

5. The caller asks about certificate details.

6. The caller asks for the exact equipment or software requirement.

7. The caller asks for a brochure, written curriculum, or official document.

8. The caller asks to compare this with another program or competitor.

9. The caller is interested but wants to speak to a human before paying.

10. The caller asks for final enrollment steps.

11. The caller is upset, distrustful, or says they were not followed up properly.

12. The caller raises a case involving special language support, accessibility, age, or unusual joining conditions.

13. The caller asks something the agent cannot answer from the loaded knowledge in one attempt.

14. The caller wants a callback at a specific time.

15. The caller wants confirmation on whether they qualify for the granted fee and needs human validation.

16. The caller asks about international timezone suitability in exact detail.

17. The caller asks for legal or invoice-related clarifications.

18. The caller asks for any batch-specific update that changes over time.
