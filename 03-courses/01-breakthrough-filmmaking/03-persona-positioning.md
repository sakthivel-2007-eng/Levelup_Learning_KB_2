# Persona Block 1 — Beginner / Student Aspiring Filmmaker

## Who this is

Someone who knows they love filmmaking but feels confused, underexposed, or directionless.

## Positioning angle for the agent

Position BFP as a structured starting point.

Emphasize that the learner does not need to have everything figured out already.

The program gives them a guided path, mentor support, live sessions, recordings, and practical execution.

## What to stress

- Weekend schedule
- Beginner-friendly structure
- Script-to-screen learning
- Live support
- Portfolio-building
- English-led delivery with possible short clarifications in other comfortable languages

## Best line of thought

> "You don’t need to start perfectly. You need structure, guidance, and consistent feedback, and that’s what this program gives you."

# Persona Block 2 — Creator / Self-Learner / Intermediate Explorer

## Who this is

Someone already making content or experimenting with visuals, but wanting to move from random creation to stronger filmmaking craft.

## Positioning angle for the agent

Position BFP as the bridge from content-making to filmmaking maturity.

Emphasize storytelling, cinematography, editing, project quality, and mentor critique.

## What to stress

- Hands-on learning
- 1:1 feedback
- Project-building
- Structured improvement
- Weekend live format
- Recordings for revision

## Best line of thought

> "If you’re already creating but want to stop depending only on trial and error, this gives you the craft, structure, and mentor feedback to level up seriously."

---

# Persona Block 3 — Working Professional / Career Switcher / Serious Hobbyist

## Who this is

Someone balancing work or another life path, but serious about moving deeper into filmmaking.

## Positioning angle for the agent

Position BFP as a practical, premium, manageable weekend program that helps them build serious filmmaking skills without needing a full-time film school setup.

## What to stress

- Weekend format
- Live plus recording access
- Practical curriculum
- Mentor feedback
- Portfolio outcome
- Structured guidance

## Best line of thought

> "If you are serious about filmmaking but need a format that fits into real life, this gives you a strong guided system without requiring you to pause everything else."
