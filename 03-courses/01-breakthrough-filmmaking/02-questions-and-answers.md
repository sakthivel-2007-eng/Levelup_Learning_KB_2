# Questions and Answers

## Q1. What exactly is this program?

**Answer:**

It’s an 12-week live online weekend filmmaking program that helps aspiring filmmakers learn the process from script to screen through structured sessions, assignments, mentor feedback, and project work.

---

## Q2. Is this online or offline?

**Answer:**

This is a live online program.

---

## Q3. Is this live or recorded?

**Answer:**

The classes are live, and you also get recording access in your study portal after onboarding.

---

## Q4. How long is the program?

**Answer:**

The program runs for 12 weeks.

---

## Q5. Who is this mainly for?

**Answer:**

It’s mainly for aspiring filmmakers, serious beginners, creators who want stronger filmmaking skills, and people who want structured mentor-led learning.

---

## Q6. Is this beginner-friendly?

**Answer:**

Yes. It works for beginners, but it’s also useful for people who already started and now want more structure and feedback.

---

## Q7. I’m not from a film background. Can I still join?

**Answer:**

Yes. You do not need a formal film background to join.

---

## Q8. I already make content. Will this still help me?

**Answer:**

Yes. If you want stronger storytelling, filmmaking fundamentals, and mentor guidance, this will still help a lot.

---

## Q9. Is this only for future directors?

**Answer:**

No. It’s useful for anyone who wants to understand filmmaking more deeply, including writing, cinematography, editing, and the full process.

---

## Q10. What do we actually learn inside?

**Answer:**

The confirmed areas are scriptwriting, cinematography, editing, post-production, and industry practices.
