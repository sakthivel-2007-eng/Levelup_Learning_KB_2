# Program Identity

## Live Breakthrough Filmmaking Program — Foundational Context

The Live Breakthrough Filmmaking Program is a 12-week live online weekend filmmaking program designed for aspiring filmmakers who want a structured path from script to screen.

This cohort is highly selective and limited to only 40 filmmakers, ensuring every participant receives meaningful mentor attention, feedback, and interaction.

It is built for people who are serious about filmmaking but do not want to learn in a random, scattered way from free content alone.

The program is hands-on and mentor-led.

Students learn through:
- Live sessions
- Assignments
- 1:1 mentor feedback
- Practical exercises
- Portfolio-building work

It covers core filmmaking areas including:
- Scriptwriting
- Cinematography
- Editing
- Post-production
- Industry practices

The goal is not just to teach concepts, but to help students apply them and create portfolio-worthy projects.

The current structure is designed to suit students and working professionals through a weekend format.

Learning sessions usually take place on Sundays from **3 PM to 7 PM**, while mentor feedback and 1:1 support sessions happen on Saturdays from **6 PM to 8 PM**.

Students also receive recording access through their study portal, typically within 24 hours after each session ends, allowing them to revisit the learning anytime.

The primary teaching language is English, though short clarifications or discussions in another language may happen if both the mentor and learner are comfortable.

Overall, the program is positioned as a premium, practical, and transformation-focused experience, built for filmmakers who want serious mentorship, real feedback, and a focused learning environment within an exclusive cohort of 40 filmmakers.
