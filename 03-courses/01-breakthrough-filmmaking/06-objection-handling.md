# Objection Handling

---

## Objection 1

### Lead

> It’s just an online course, why pay even an application fee?

### Rep

> I understand that reaction. But BFP is not a recorded course library. The whole point is cohort-based learning with live critique, deadlines, feedback, and mentor interaction. That’s why the interview stage matters. The fee just secures access to that process.

---

## Objection 2

### Lead

> I’m not sure filmmaking can be learned online.

### Rep

> That’s exactly why this program is structured around doing, not just watching. The notes in your second brain mention live demos, screen-share breakdowns, real-time feedback, and remote short-film execution. The format is built to make online still highly practical.

---

## Objection 3

### Lead

> My weekends are packed.

### Rep

> Fair. But that’s also why the program is weekend-led and weekdays are left for self-paced assignment work. The interview is where we check schedule fit properly. Right now the question is only whether you want to keep your spot in the process.

---

## Objection 4

### Lead

> I don’t have fancy gear.

### Rep

> You don’t need it to apply. The BFP FAQ itself says a phone shooting 1080p is enough to get started. The interview is to assess your seriousness, not whether you already own professional gear.

---

## Objection 5

### Lead

> I’m not good enough yet.

### Rep

> The selection is based on passion and consistency, not perfection. Even the FAQ calls out short scripts, YouTube edits, or phone films as valid signals. So don’t disqualify yourself before the interview.
