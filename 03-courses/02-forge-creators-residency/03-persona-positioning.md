# Persona Positioning

---

# Persona 1 — Founder / Operator / Builder

## Who they are

Someone building a business, product, service, or career who knows they should be more visible online but has not built a consistent personal brand.

### What they usually feel

- "I know content matters, but I don't know what to say."
- "I'm building something real, but I'm invisible online."
- "I don't want shallow content. I want content that actually represents me."

### How the agent should position the residency

Frame the residency as a personal-brand acceleration environment.

This is not about becoming an influencer for ego.

It is about building authority, trust, visibility, and narrative around what they are already building.

Emphasize:

- Clarity
- Expression
- Consistency
- Being surrounded by ambitious people

### Key selling angle

> "This helps you stop hiding behind the work and start becoming visible with intention."

---

# Persona 2 — Early-Stage Creator / Aspiring Creator

## Who they are

Someone who wants to create seriously but still feels confused, inconsistent, or underconfident. They may not have a clear niche yet and may not have posted much.

### What they usually feel

- "I know I want to create, but I don't know where to start."
- "I overthink every post."
- "I want to find my voice and actually become consistent."

### How the agent should position the residency

Frame the residency as a clarity, confidence, and action experience.

The biggest benefit is not advanced tactics.

It is getting unstuck, learning in a real environment, and starting to create with support and momentum.

### Key selling angle

> "This gives you the push, structure, and environment that most people never get when they try to do it alone."

---

# Persona 3 — Stuck Creator / Freelancer / Working Professional

## Who they are

Someone already doing some work or posting some content, but not in a focused or intentional way.

They may feel scattered, inconsistent, or unseen.

### What they usually feel

- "I've started, but I'm not really building anything meaningful."
- "I post sometimes, then disappear."
- "I know I have potential, but I'm not able to convert that into momentum."

### How the agent should position the residency

Frame the residency as a reset and realignment.

This is for people who do not need more random tips.

They need space, feedback, ambition around them, and a serious environment that helps them reconnect with why they are creating in the first place.

### Key selling angle

> "This is not about starting from zero. It is about finally becoming intentional with the creator journey you already began."
