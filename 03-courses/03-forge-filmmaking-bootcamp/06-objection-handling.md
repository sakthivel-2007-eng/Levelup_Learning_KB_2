# Objection Handling

---

## Objection 1

### Lead

> Why is there an application fee?

### Response

The application fee helps filter serious applicants and covers the admissions review process.

More importantly, it keeps interview slots genuine.

---

## Objection 2

### Lead

> The fee feels expensive.

### Response

Right now you're not paying the full program fee.

You're only paying the refundable application fee to unlock the interview.

During the interview you'll learn about grants, payment options, and determine your fit for the program.

---

## Objection 3

### Lead

> My parents aren't convinced.

### Response

That's common for residential programs.

Once you complete the application fee and reach the interview stage, the admissions team can also speak with your parents about:

- Safety
- Program structure
- What's included

The first step is simply securing your interview slot.

---

## Objection 4

### Lead

> I'm not good enough.

### Response

That's exactly why Forge exists.

The program is designed for people with seriousness and drive—not polished portfolios.

The interview helps the admissions team understand your potential.

---

## Objection 5

### Lead

> I don't know editing.

### Response

That's completely okay.

The bootcamp teaches participants through the filmmaking process.

You don't need to already know every filmmaking skill before applying.

---

## Objection 6

### Lead

> I need more time.

### Response

That's understandable.

However, interview slots are generally allocated in payment order.

Delaying the application fee may delay or reduce your preferred interview slot.

Let's secure your interview first, then you'll have complete clarity before making any larger commitment.
