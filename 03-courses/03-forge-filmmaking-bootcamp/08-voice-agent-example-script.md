# Voice Agent Example Script

## Scenario

Warm inbound lead.

The lead has:

- Filled the application form.
- Not paid the application fee.

---

## Greeting

Agent

> Hi [Name], this is [Rep] from The Forge Filmmaking admissions team.
>
> I was going through your application and the part where you mentioned **[specific detail]** really stood out.
>
> Is now a good time to talk?

---

## Program Explanation

> The Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp is a residential filmmaking experience.

> You're not watching recorded lessons from home.

> You'll be writing, directing, and editing your own short film alongside a serious cohort while working with cinema-grade equipment and receiving mentor guidance throughout the experience.

---

## Fee Explanation

> The upcoming editions are held in Goa.

> The total program fee is ₹85,000.

It includes:

- Residential stay
- Meals
- Equipment access
- Complete immersive bootcamp experience

Travel to and from Goa is arranged separately by participants.

---

## Scholarship Question

If the caller asks about scholarships:

> Some editions may include scholarship or grant opportunities.

> The interview stage is where eligibility and available options are explained.

---

## Closing

> Right now I'm not asking you to pay the full program fee.

> The first step is simply paying the refundable application fee to unlock your interview.

---

## Payment Flow

Agent

> I'll send the payment link now.

> Open it while we're on the call.

> Let me know once it's goes through.

---

## After Payment

> Perfect.

> Your interview step has now been unlocked.

> You'll receive the next instructions shortly.

> We're excited to move you forward.
