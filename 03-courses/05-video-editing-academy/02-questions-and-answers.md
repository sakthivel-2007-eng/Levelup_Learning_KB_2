# Questions and Answers

## Question 1

### What exactly is the Video Editing Academy?

It is a 12-week live online Video Editing Academy by LevelUp Learning designed to help learners build professional editing skills through live classes, mentor feedback, structured practice, and practical projects.

---

## Question 2

### Is this a live course or just recordings?

The academy is primarily a live cohort. Recordings are provided for revision, but the main value comes from live teaching, mentor interaction, accountability, and feedback.

---

## Question 3

### How long is the program?

The program runs for **12 weeks**.

---

## Question 4

### How many days a week are classes conducted?

The academy follows a **weekend format**, typically with sessions on **Saturday and Sunday**.

## Question 6
