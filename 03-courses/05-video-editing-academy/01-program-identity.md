# Program Identity

## Overview

LevelUp Live Video Editing Academy is a live online, weekend-based video editing cohort designed for people who want to become skilled, job-ready video editors through structured learning, mentor feedback, and consistent practice.

This is not simply a library of recorded videos. It is a guided learning experience built around live classes, projects, reviews, and accountability.

---

## Program Format

- Live online cohort
- 12-week duration
- Weekend learning model
- Mentor-led sessions
- Practical assignments
- Continuous feedback

Typical schedule:

- Saturday: Mentor review and feedback sessions
- Sunday: Core learning sessions

Exact timings should always be confirmed with the admissions team.

---

## Who It Is For

The academy is intended for people who want to learn video editing seriously, including:

- Beginners
- College students
- Working professionals
- Freelancers
- Career switchers
- Content creators

---

## Core Promise

The academy is designed to help learners:

- Build strong editing fundamentals
- Learn through live guidance
- Complete practical projects
- Receive mentor feedback
- Build confidence through structured practice
- Develop a professional portfolio

The goal is not simply to watch videos, but to improve continuously through guided learning and accountability.

---

## Learning Experience

Students participate in:

- Live teaching
- Mentor reviews
- Practical assignments
- Portfolio projects
- Continuous feedback

Recordings are also provided after onboarding through the student study portal for revision.

---

## Language

Classes are conducted in English.

---

## Learning Outcome

By the end of the cohort, learners are expected to have:

- Stronger editing skills
- Better creative decision-making
- A portfolio of practical work
- Increased confidence
- A structured foundation for professional growth
