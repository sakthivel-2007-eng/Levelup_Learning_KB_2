# Forge Writing Retreat — Objection Handling

This guide contains approved responses for the most common objections raised by prospective applicants.

---

## Objection: "₹600 even to apply feels unnecessary."

### Response

I completely understand.

The ₹600 isn't a random fee. Since Forge Writing Retreat is a selective admissions program, it acts as the first commitment step and confirms your interview slot.

If you're not selected after the interview, the application fee is refundable.

---

## Objection: "I write screenplays, not books."

### Response

That's completely fine.

Forge Writing Retreat welcomes both screenwriters and authors.

The mentorship focuses on storytelling fundamentals such as character, dialogue, structure, pacing, and scene construction, making it relevant regardless of your writing format.

---

## Objection: "I'm not sure it's worth it."

### Response

That's a fair concern.

At this stage you're not committing to the entire retreat.

You're simply unlocking your interview, where you'll receive complete clarity about the program, determine if it's the right fit, and ask every question before making a larger decision.

---

## Objection: "I can't take six days off."

### Response

That's understandable.

The first step is simply securing your interview slot.

If you're shortlisted, the admissions team will discuss cohort timing and help you determine whether the retreat fits your schedule.

Don't rule yourself out before you've even been evaluated.

---

## Objection: "My family won't understand why I need this."

### Response

That happens more often than you might think with creative programs.

Once you reach the interview stage, the admissions team can explain the seriousness of the retreat, its structure, and expected outcomes so you have the information needed for that conversation.

For now, the best step is simply getting into the admissions process.
