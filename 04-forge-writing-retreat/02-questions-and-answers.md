# Forge Writing Retreat - Questions & Answers

## Question 1

### What exactly is Forge Writing Retreat?

It is a 6-day residential writing retreat for screenwriters, authors, and serious storytellers who want focused time, mentorship, and a strong creative environment to work on their writing.

---

## Question 2

### Is this a course or a retreat?

It is a retreat-led learning experience. You do learn craft, but the bigger idea is immersion, writing time, mentor feedback, and being in a serious storytelling environment.

---

## Question 3

### Is this online or offline?

This is an offline residential program.

---

## Question 4

### How many days is it?

The Writing Retreat is structured as a 6-day immersive residency.

---

## Question 5

### Who is this meant for?

It is meant for authors, screenwriters, aspiring writers, and people who have a story inside them but need the right structure and environment to develop it.
