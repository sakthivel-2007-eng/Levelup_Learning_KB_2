# Forge Writing Retreat — Abandoned Lead Script

## Use Case

Use this script when:

- The lead has filled out the application form.
- The lead has **not** completed the ₹600 application fee.
- The goal is to understand the hesitation, explain the value of the program, and encourage them to complete the application fee to unlock their interview.

---

# Main Conversation Flow

## Opening

**Rep:**

> Hi **[Name]**, this is **[Rep]** from The Forge Writing admissions team.
>
> I was reading your application, and what you wrote about **[insert a specific writing goal or pain point from the form]** really stood out.

---

**Rep:**

> I noticed you filled the form but haven't completed the application fee yet, so I wanted to quickly check — what held you back?

---

**Lead responds**

Listen completely before responding.

---

## Value Positioning

**Rep:**

> Understood.
>
> Just to give you the right context — this isn't a generic writing workshop.
>
> It's a **selective residential writing retreat** built for serious writers who want focused writing time, mentor feedback, and actual progress on their work.
>
> The structure is designed around:
>
> - Learning sessions
> - Writing blocks
> - One-to-one mentorship
>
> It is not passive listening.

---

## Explain Why the Program Is Different

**Rep:**

> For many people, the biggest value is finally getting uninterrupted space to write properly.
>
> Along with that, participants receive:
>
> - Mentor guidance
> - A serious writing community
> - Publishing-facing exposure
>
> The application fee simply locks your interview slot.
>
> If you're not selected, it is refundable.

---

## Soft Close

**Rep:**

> I can send the application fee link now and help you secure your interview in one go.

---

# Objection Handling

## Objection 1

### Lead

> ₹600 also feels unnecessary.

### Rep

> I completely understand.
>
> It isn't a random charge.
>
> Since the program is selective, the application fee helps ensure only serious applicants move into the interview stage.
>
> It's also refundable if you're not selected.

---

## Objection 2

### Lead

> I write screenplays, not books.

### Rep

> That's completely fine.
>
> Forge Writing Retreat supports both screenwriters and authors.
>
> The mentoring also covers storytelling craft like character, dialogue, scene work, and structure, so the interview is still highly relevant for you.

---

## Objection 3

### Lead

> I'm not sure it's worth it.

### Rep

> That's fair.
>
> Right now you're not committing to the full program.
>
> You're simply unlocking your interview, where you can understand the program properly, ask questions, and determine whether it's the right fit.

---

## Objection 4

### Lead

> I can't take six days off.

### Rep

> That's a valid concern.
>
> But the immediate step is only securing your interview slot.
>
> If you're shortlisted, the admissions team can discuss the cohort schedule and practical planning with you before any final decision.

---

## Objection 5

### Lead

> My family won't understand why I need this.

### Rep

> That happens quite often with creative programs.
>
> Once you reach the interview stage, the admissions team can explain the seriousness of the program, its structure, and expected outcomes more clearly.
>
> Let's first get you into that process.

---

# Closing the Call

## Rep

> I'll send the application fee link right now.
>

---

## Rep

> Once you complete the payment, your interview gets unlocked.
>
> Tell me when the payment page opens.

>
> You're now through to the interview stage.
>
