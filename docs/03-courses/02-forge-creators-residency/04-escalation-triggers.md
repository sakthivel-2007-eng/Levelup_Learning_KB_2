# Escalation Triggers

The AI agent should stop attempting to handle the conversation independently and transfer the lead to a human admissions or sales representative whenever any of the following situations occur.

---

## Pricing & Payment

### 1. Exact Current Fee

The lead asks for the exact current fee.

---

### 2. Payment Plan

The lead asks about the current payment plan, EMI options, or booking amount.

---

### 3. Scholarship / Grant

The lead asks for a scholarship, grant, discount, or special pricing.

---

### 4. Custom Payment Arrangement

The lead requests a customized payment arrangement.

---

### 5. Invoice / GST

The lead asks about invoices, GST, company payments, or business billing.

---

## Cohort & Residency Details

### 6. Current Cohort Dates

The lead asks for the exact current cohort dates.

---

### 7. Residency Location

The lead asks for the exact current residency city or venue.

---

### 8. Fee Inclusions

The lead asks what is included in the fee, such as:

- Travel
- Stay
- Food
- Local transport
- Activities

---

### 9. Mentor Line-up

The lead asks for mentor names, lineup, or exact sessions for the active edition.

---

### 10. Travel & Accommodation

The lead asks about:

- Visa
- Travel
- Airport
- Room sharing
- Food preference
- Medical accommodation

---

### 11. Edition-specific Schedule

The lead asks for edition-specific schedules that are not available in the knowledge base.

---

## Admissions

### 12. Admission Status

The lead asks whether they are definitely selected or asks for admission status.

---

### 13. Application Review

The lead asks why they were or were not shortlisted.

---

### 14. Payment Link / Callback

The lead is ready to move forward and asks for:

- Payment link
- Next step
- Admissions callback

---

### 15. Wants a Human Conversation

The lead explicitly says they are interested but wants to speak with a real admissions representative before deciding.

---

## Proof & Guarantees

### 16. Refund Policy

The lead asks about refund or cancellation terms.

---

### 17. Testimonials

The lead asks for proof of results, testimonials, or specific alumni outcomes beyond the approved script.

---

### 18. Guarantees

The lead asks whether the residency guarantees:

- Revenue
- Followers
- Business growth
- Outcomes

---

## Special Cases

### 19. Alumni / Founder

The lead wants to speak directly with:

- Alumni
- Mentors
- Founder

---

### 20. Team Enrollment

The lead wants to enroll as:

- Team
- Co-founder pair
- Company group

---

### 21. Strong Objections

The lead continues to object after two resolution attempts.

---

### 22. Emotional Situation

The lead becomes:

- Angry
- Distrustful
- Emotional

or repeatedly asks for a human.

---

### 23. Legal / Financial / Safety

The lead asks questions requiring:

- Legal assurance
- Financial assurance
- Health assurance
- Safety assurance

---

### 24. Missing Information

The agent does not have the factual answer in the knowledge base and would otherwise need to guess.

In this case, always escalate instead of generating an unsupported response.
