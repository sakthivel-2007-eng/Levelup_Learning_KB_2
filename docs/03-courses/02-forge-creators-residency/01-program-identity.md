# Program Identity

## Program Name

Forge Creators Residencies

---

## Overview

Forge Creators Residencies is an invite-only, high-touch creator residency limited to just 24 creators.

It is designed for founders, builders, and creators who want to build a real personal brand through content—not just post randomly and hope it works.

The program is built for people who want clarity on what they stand for, what kind of content they should create, how to create it consistently, and how to grow within a serious creator community.

Because the residency is intentionally limited to only 24 creators, every participant receives deeper interaction, meaningful feedback, and close collaboration within a small, focused cohort of ambitious peers.

This is not a passive course.

It is an immersive creation experience built around learning directly from experienced creators, making real content, receiving feedback, and living alongside a like-minded community of driven individuals.

The program blends:

- Strategy
- Execution
- Personal brand thinking
- Content creation practice
- Community

Depending on the edition, the residency may include pre-residency preparation followed by an in-person residency in a destination setting, where creators step away from their usual environment and focus fully on building their voice and output.

---

## Core Promise

Help a creator move from confusion and inconsistency to clarity, action, and visible output.

---

## Best Suited For

- Founders who want to become more visible online
- Creators seeking stronger identity and consistency
- Ambitious people who know they should be creating but have been trying to do it alone

---

## Positioning

Forge Creators Residencies is positioned as a transformational creator experience—not just a workshop, a course, or a vacation.

It is a focused, high-intent environment where 24 serious creators come together to build, create, and grow.
