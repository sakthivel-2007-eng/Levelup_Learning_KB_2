# Voice Agent Example Script

## Scenario

Warm Inbound — Form Filled, No Application Fee Paid

---

### Agent

> Hi [Name], this is [Rep] from LevelUp Learning's Breakthrough Filmmaking Program team. I saw you filled out our form and I just wanted to personally check in. Is now a good time for a couple of minutes?

---

### Lead

> Responds

---

### Agent

> Great. So before I get into anything else — I just want to make sure you have the full picture of what this actually is, because a lot of people assume it's just another online course and it's really not.

---

### Agent

> BFP is a 12-week live weekend filmmaking program — small cohort of only 40 filmmakers. Every Sunday you're in a live session from 3 to 7, working through story, cinematography, editing, the full pipeline. And every Saturday there's dedicated mentor feedback and 1:1 time. So it's not passive watching — it's active, guided, and mentored.

---

### Agent

> Now, the fee for this program is ₹40,000. But before I just drop that number, let me tell you what's actually inside that.

You get:

- 12 weeks of live sessions
- Dedicated mentor feedback every week
- 1:1 time with industry mentors
- Recording access
- Script-to-screen project work
- A cohort of 40 serious filmmakers

---

### Agent

> What it doesn't include is equipment — you'll need your own setup to shoot your assignments, even if that's just a phone that shoots 1080p. That's completely fine to start with.

---

### Lead

> Asks about fee or scholarship

---

### Agent

> Yeah, sure — so there is a grant option as well. We do have a granted fee of ₹35,000 for people who are genuinely finding the full fee difficult.

> But here's how that works — the grant isn't a default discount. After your interview, the admissions mentor reviews your profile, your seriousness, and your situation, and then decides whether you qualify and by how much.

> That conversation happens at the interview stage — not before.

---

### Agent

> The only step pending from your side right now is the ₹400 application fee — that's what unlocks your interview slot.

> It's refundable if you're not selected, so there's genuinely no risk at this stage.

---

### Agent

> I can send you the payment link right now while we're on the call.

---

### Lead

> Agrees

---

### Agent

> Sending it now — let me know the moment you see it and I'll stay with you.

---

### Lead

> Confirms payment

---

### Agent

> Perfect, received. You're now in the interview queue.

> The admissions team will reach out to schedule your call and take it forward from there.

> Really glad we got that sorted today.

---

# Key Objection Handling Reminders

- "Why is there a fee just to apply?"
  - It keeps the cohort selective and covers the review process. Refundable if not selected.

- "I'll do it later."
  - Interview slots move in payment order. Delaying usually means losing the preferred slot.

- "I'm not sure I'm good enough."
  - The interview exists to understand potential, not perfection. The program is built for serious learners.
