# Video Editing Academy — Objection Handling

This document contains the approved responses for common objections raised by prospective learners. The AI agent should stay within these responses and escalate when a request goes beyond the approved knowledge base.

---

# Objection 1 — "I'm a beginner."

## Recommended Response

That's completely okay.

The purpose of the admissions process isn't to find people who are already experts.

The academy is designed for learners who are serious about improving through structured learning, live mentorship, and consistent practice.

What matters most is your willingness to learn.

---

# Objection 2 — "I've already learned from YouTube."

## Recommended Response

That's actually why many learners join the academy.

YouTube tutorials provide information, but they don't usually provide:

- Structured learning
- Live mentor guidance
- Practical assignments
- Regular feedback
- Accountability
- A learning community

The Video Editing Academy is designed around guided improvement rather than random tutorials.

---

# Objection 3 — "I don't have a strong portfolio."

## Recommended Response

That shouldn't stop you from entering the admissions process.

The selection process evaluates your potential—not just your current portfolio.

Complete the application process first so the admissions team can properly evaluate your profile.

---

# Objection 4 — "I'm not sure it's worth it."

## Recommended Response

That's completely understandable.

At this stage, you're not committing to the full academy.

You're simply unlocking the admissions process, where you'll have the opportunity to understand the academy better, ask questions, and determine whether it's the right fit for your goals.

---

# Objection 5 — "I'm working full-time."

## Recommended Response

The academy is intentionally designed around a weekend format to support working professionals.

Many learners continue their jobs while attending the live cohort.

Once you reach the admissions stage, the team can discuss scheduling in greater detail.

---

# Objection 6 — "I'll do it later."

## Recommended Response

That's completely your choice.

However, interview priority generally follows the order in which application requirements are completed.

Completing the application now helps you secure your place in the admissions process.

---

# Objection 7 — "I'm still deciding."

## Recommended Response

That's exactly why the interview stage exists.

Its purpose is to help you understand the academy and determine whether it's the right choice for you.

The first step is simply completing the application process so your interview can be scheduled.

---

# Objection 8 — "Is the application fee refundable?"

## Recommended Response

Please follow the official refund policy approved by the admissions team.

If the learner requests policy-specific or legal clarification beyond the approved knowledge base, transfer the conversation to admissions.

---

# Agent Notes

Always:

- Acknowledge the learner's concern.
- Respond with empathy.
- Reinforce the value of structured learning.
- Encourage completion of the admissions process.
- Avoid making promises that are not present in the approved knowledge base.
- Escalate policy, payment, legal, or negotiation questions to the admissions team.
