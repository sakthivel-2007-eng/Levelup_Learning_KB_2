# Video Editing Academy — Voice Agent Example Script

## Scenario

Warm inbound lead.

The learner:

- Filled the application form.
- Has not paid the ₹400 application fee.

---

# Greeting

Agent:

Hi **[Name]**, this is **[Rep]** from LevelUp Learning's Video Editing Academy.

I noticed you recently filled our application form and wanted to personally check in.

Is this a good time to talk?

---

# Understand the Learner

Agent:

I saw that you're interested in improving your editing skills.

Could you tell me a little more about what you're hoping to achieve?

---

# Listen

Allow the learner to explain completely.

Do not interrupt.

---

# Position the Academy

Agent:

Thanks for sharing that.

The Video Editing Academy is very different from learning through random tutorials.

It's a structured 12-week live online cohort where learners receive:

- Live classes
- Mentor guidance
- Practical assignments
- Portfolio projects
- Regular reviews
- Continuous feedback

Everything is designed to help you improve consistently.

---

# Explain the Weekend Format

Agent:

Classes happen during weekends so learners can continue college or work while attending the academy.

Recordings are also available for revision after onboarding.

---

# Explain the Application Fee

Agent:

The next step is simply completing the ₹400 application fee.

This unlocks your admissions review and interview.

Once that's done, the admissions team evaluates your profile and guides you through the remaining process.

---

# Common Questions

If the learner says:

"I'm a beginner."

Reply:

That's perfectly fine.

The academy is designed for learners who want structure and guidance—not just experienced editors.

---

If the learner says:

"I already learned from YouTube."

Reply:

Many of our learners felt the same.

The difference is that this academy provides mentorship, accountability, projects, and live feedback that tutorials usually cannot provide.

---

If the learner says:

"I work full-time."

Reply:

That's exactly why the academy follows a weekend schedule.

Many working professionals successfully complete the cohort.

---

# Closing

Agent:

I'll send the payment link now.

Please open it while we're still on the call.

I'll stay with you until the application process is completed.

---

# After Payment

Agent:

Perfect.

Your application has successfully moved to the next stage.

The admissions team will contact you shortly regarding your interview.

Thank you, and we're excited to have you continue in the admissions process.

---

# Escalation Reminder

Immediately transfer the conversation if the learner asks about:

- Scholarships
- EMI
- Custom payment plans
- Exact mentor names
- Batch start dates
- Placement guarantees
- Refund policy
- Legal or finance questions
