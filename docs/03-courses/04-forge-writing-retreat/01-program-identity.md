# Forge Writing Retreat

## Program Overview

Forge Writing Retreat is a **6-day residential writing retreat** designed for screenwriters, authors, and serious storytellers who want to step away from the distractions of everyday life and dedicate focused time to their writing.

Rather than functioning as a lecture-based workshop, the retreat provides an immersive environment centered around writing, reflection, mentorship, feedback, and community.

---

# Purpose

The retreat exists to help participants reconnect with their stories by giving them the time, structure, and creative environment that everyday life often makes difficult.

Whether someone has:

- Been holding onto an idea for years,
- Become stuck midway through a script or manuscript,
- Or wants deeper guidance on an existing project,

the retreat is designed to help them move forward with clarity and momentum.

---

# Learning Experience

Participants spend six days inside a residential environment where writing becomes the primary focus.

The experience combines:

- Dedicated writing sessions
- Reflection and creative exploration
- Mentor-led guidance
- Constructive feedback
- Independent writing time
- Community discussions
- Shared learning with fellow storytellers

Instead of consuming information passively, participants actively develop their own stories throughout the retreat.

---

# What Makes Forge Different

Forge Writing Retreat is positioned as more than a traditional writing workshop.

Its unique strengths include:

- Residential immersive format
- Protected creative environment
- Serious writing community
- Guided mentorship
- Focus on meaningful progress
- Emotional as well as technical growth

The emphasis is not only on improving writing skills but also on helping participants rediscover their creative voice.

---

# Mentorship

Participants receive guidance from experienced writers throughout the retreat.

Mentorship focuses on:

- Story development
- Creative direction
- Constructive feedback
- Writing process
- Personal growth as a storyteller

The exact mentor lineup may vary between cohorts.

---

# Community Experience

One of the defining characteristics of Forge Writing Retreat is its community.

Participants live and write alongside other serious storytellers who are equally committed to their creative work.

This shared environment encourages:

- Collaboration
- Honest conversations
- Creative accountability
- Mutual encouragement
- Long-term creative relationships

---

# Who Is It For?

Forge Writing Retreat is designed for:

- Screenwriters
- Authors
- Aspiring writers
- Storytellers
- Writers with unfinished projects
- People holding onto story ideas
- Working professionals seeking dedicated writing time
- Serious beginners willing to commit to the writing process

---

# Core Promise

Forge Writing Retreat promises to provide the environment, structure, mentorship, and community that help participants reconnect with their stories.

Rather than promising publication or commercial success, the retreat focuses on helping writers develop:

- Creative clarity
- Writing confidence
- Stronger storytelling
- Consistent momentum
- A deeper relationship with their work

The goal is for participants to leave with meaningful progress on their writing and a renewed commitment to their creative journey.
