# Forge Writing Retreat — Escalation Triggers

This document defines situations where the AI admissions agent **must immediately stop handling the conversation independently** and transfer the lead to a human admissions counselor.

---

# Escalate Immediately If:

## 1. Pricing Information

The caller asks for:

- Exact current program fee
- Updated fee after recent changes

…and the loaded knowledge base has not been updated.

---

## 2. Payment Structure

The caller asks about:

- EMI options
- Installment amounts
- Payment deadlines
- Current payment structure

that require live admissions information.

---

## 3. Scholarships or Discounts

The caller asks about:

- Scholarships
- Grants
- Discounts
- Fee waivers
- Fee negotiation

---

## 4. Batch Logistics

The caller asks for information not confirmed in the live admissions sheet, including:

- Cohort dates
- Final venue
- Room-sharing details
- Travel logistics

---

## 5. Mentor Information

The caller asks for:

- Mentor names
- Mentor biographies
- Credentials
- Whether a specific mentor is teaching the current cohort

---

## 6. Refund & Policy Questions

The caller asks about:

- Refund policy
- Cancellation policy
- Transfer policy
- Deferral policy

---

## 7. Medical or Accessibility Needs

The caller has questions regarding:

- Medical requirements
- Accessibility
- Dietary needs
- Special accommodation requests

---

## 8. Admission Eligibility Edge Cases

The caller asks whether they qualify based on:

- Age
- Nationality
- Language
- Other exceptional admission criteria

---

## 9. Writing Sample Review

The caller wants:

- Feedback on their writing sample
- Profile evaluation
- Chances of selection
- Personalized admission assessment

---

## 10. Genre or Language Support

The caller asks whether a specific:

- Genre
- Writing format
- Language

is supported when that information is not explicitly available in the knowledge base.

---

## 11. Difficult Conversations

Escalate if the caller:

- Becomes angry
- Becomes distrustful
- Is repeatedly confused
- Challenges the agent

---

## 12. Human Request

Immediately transfer if the caller says they want to:

- Speak to a human
- Talk with admissions directly

---

## 13. Existing Admission Issues

Escalate if the caller says they:

- Already paid
- Already attended an interview
- Were promised something previously
- Have an unresolved admissions issue

---

## 14. Legal or Complaint Issues

Transfer immediately if the caller mentions:

- Refund disputes
- Formal complaints
- Legal action
- Legal language

---

## 15. Commercial Guarantees

The caller asks for guarantees regarding:

- Publishing
- Production
- Career placement
- Commercial success

beyond what the approved knowledge base allows.

---

## 16. Sensitive Emotional Situations

Immediately escalate if the caller shares:

- Mental health concerns
- Crisis situations
- Serious emotional distress

beyond ordinary hesitation.

---

## 17. Live Seat Availability

The caller asks for:

- Current seat availability
- Remaining seats
- Live admissions capacity

---

## 18. Third-Party Verification

The caller is:

- A parent
- A guardian
- A family member
- Another representative

requesting enrollment information on behalf of someone else.

---

## 19. Billing Support

The caller requests:

- GST invoice
- Company invoice
- Employer reimbursement
- Corporate billing support

---

## 20. Ready-to-Enroll Leads

The caller is highly interested and:

- Ready to complete admissions
- Needs exact next-step guidance
- Requires immediate enrollment assistance

Transfer to a human admissions counselor so the enrollment process can be completed smoothly.

---

# Agent Rule

Whenever a question depends on **live, batch-specific, policy-specific, pricing-specific, or commercially sensitive information**, **never guess**.

Always transfer the conversation to a human admissions team member.
