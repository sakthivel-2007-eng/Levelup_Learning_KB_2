# Questions and Answers

---

# A. Program Basics

## Q1. What exactly is Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp?

It is an immersive residential filmmaking bootcamp where you learn by actually making films, not just by watching lessons.

---

## Q2. Is this a course, a retreat, or a workshop?

It has the intensity of a bootcamp, the immersion of a retreat, and the practical learning of a workshop, but its primary focus is filmmaking through real execution.

---

## Q3. Is it online or offline?

It is a hybrid experience consisting of **7 days of online learning** followed by **8 days of in-person residential training**.

---

## Q4. What makes this different from a normal filmmaking class?

A normal class teaches filmmaking.

Forge places you inside a real filmmaking environment where you collaborate, create, receive feedback, and learn through practical execution.

---

## Q5. Will I actually make something during the bootcamp?

Yes.

The bootcamp is built around practical output, real filmmaking exercises, and creating meaningful film work during the program.

---

## Q6. Is it mostly theory or mostly practical?

It is heavily practical.

Theory is taught only to support execution during the online learning phase.

---

## Q7. What is the main outcome of the bootcamp?

Participants gain:

- Real filmmaking experience
- Stronger creative clarity
- Mentor feedback
- Hands-on exposure
- Portfolio growth through creating their own short film

---

## Q8. Why do people join Forge instead of just learning from YouTube?

Because YouTube can teach information.

Forge provides:

- Structure
- Mentor feedback
- Immersion
- Collaboration
- Accountability
- A real filmmaking environment

---
