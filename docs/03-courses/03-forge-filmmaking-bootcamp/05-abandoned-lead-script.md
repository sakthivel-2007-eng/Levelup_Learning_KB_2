# Abandoned Lead Script

## Use Case

Use this script when:

- The lead has submitted the application form.
- The application fee (₹800–₹900) has not been paid.
- The interview slot has not yet been unlocked.

---

## Opening

**Rep**

> Hi [Name], this is [Rep] from The Forge Filmmaking admissions team.
>
> I was just going through your application, and the part where you mentioned **[insert one specific detail from their form]** really stood out to us.

---

**Rep**

> I noticed you completed the application form but haven't locked your interview slot by paying the application fee.
>
> I just wanted to quickly check in—what stopped you?

---

## If the Lead Responds

**Rep**

> Got it.
>
> Just so you have complete clarity, Forge isn't a theory-heavy workshop.
>
> It's a selective residential filmmaking bootcamp where you actually write, direct, and edit your own short film.
>
> You'll also work on other projects, collaborate with practicing filmmakers, and use cinema-grade equipment inside a very small cohort.
>
> That's why the interview process is selective.

---

**Rep**

> The application fee simply locks your interview slot and shows seriousness.
>
> If you're not selected, it's fully refundable.
>
> Once the payment is completed, your interview step opens immediately.

---

**Rep**

> I can send the payment link right now while we're on the call.
>
> It takes less than a minute.
>
> Open it once and I'll stay with you until it's completed.

---

## Goal

Move the lead from:

Application Submitted

↓

Application Fee Paid

↓

Interview Slot Unlocked
