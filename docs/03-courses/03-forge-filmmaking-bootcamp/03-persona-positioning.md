# Persona Positioning

This guide helps admissions agents and AI voice agents position the Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp differently based on the caller's background, goals, and concerns.

---

# Persona 1 — Aspiring Beginner

## Who They Are

- Student
- Fresher
- Young creator
- Film enthusiast
- Someone who has always wanted to make films but has never had structured guidance

---

## What They Usually Care About

- "Will I be able to keep up?"
- "Do I need experience?"
- "Will this finally help me start filmmaking seriously?"

---

## Positioning Strategy

Present Forge as the fastest way for a serious beginner to move from watching filmmaking to actually making films.

Emphasize that applicants do not need film school experience. What matters most is commitment, seriousness, and a willingness to learn through practice.

---

## Key Points to Emphasize

- Beginner-friendly for serious learners
- Practical learning instead of passive theory
- Real mentor guidance
- Continuous feedback
- Supportive peer community
- Strong first step into filmmaking

---

## Avoid Saying

- That the program is only for experienced filmmakers
- Technical jargon that may intimidate beginners
- Anything that guarantees employment or career outcomes

---

## Suggested Closing

> "If you've been waiting for the right environment to properly begin your filmmaking journey, Forge is built for exactly that turning point."

---

# Persona 2 — Career Switcher / Creator

## Who They Are

- Working professional
- Content creator
- Editor
- Marketer
- Freelancer
- Creator looking to move into filmmaking more seriously

---

## What They Usually Care About

- "Is taking time off worth it?"
- "Will this be more than just motivation?"
- "Can this actually help me transition into filmmaking?"

---

## Positioning Strategy

Position Forge as an immersive creative reset rather than another short-term creative event.

Explain that the bootcamp combines learning, collaboration, and practical execution into one focused filmmaking experience.

---

## Key Points to Emphasize

- High-intensity immersion
- Practical filmmaking experience
- Creative clarity and direction
- Living and working alongside serious storytellers
- Ideal for creators transitioning from content creation into filmmaking

---

## Avoid Saying

- That it is simply a relaxing getaway
- That the program is low intensity
- That it is designed only for college students

---

## Suggested Closing

> "If you already know filmmaking is something you want to pursue seriously, Forge helps you stop postponing that transition."

---

# Persona 3 — Emerging Filmmaker

## Who They Are

- Early-stage director
- Cinematographer
- Writer
- Independent filmmaker
- Someone who has already created small projects but wants greater creative growth

---

## What They Usually Care About

- "Will I still benefit if I've already started?"
- "Is the cohort serious?"
- "Will the mentors challenge me?"

---

## Positioning Strategy

Position Forge as an opportunity to sharpen craft inside a serious creative ecosystem rather than repeating filmmaking basics.

Focus on immersive practice, peer collaboration, mentor guidance, and creative acceleration.

---

## Key Points to Emphasize

- Serious cohort quality
- Practical mentor guidance
- Intensive creative immersion
- Better collaboration
- Stronger creative discipline
- Increased momentum and output

---

## Avoid Saying

- That it is only an introductory hobby program
- Basic filmmaking explanations unless requested
- That the program guarantees industry success

---

## Suggested Closing

> "If you've already started filmmaking but feel you need a stronger creative ecosystem and a more serious push, Forge is where that acceleration can happen."
