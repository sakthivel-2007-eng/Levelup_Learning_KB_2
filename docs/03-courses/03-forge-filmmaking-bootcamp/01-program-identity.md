# Program Identity

## Program Overview

Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp is a premium, residential, immersive filmmaking experience by Forge / LevelUp Learning for people who want to stop only learning about filmmaking and start actually making films.

The core philosophy is simple:

**Learn by doing.**

Participants live and create with a serious cohort of storytellers in a destination setting while gaining practical experience throughout the filmmaking process.

---

## Purpose

The bootcamp is designed to help participants move beyond theory by immersing them in real filmmaking practice.

Instead of passively consuming lessons, participants actively create films while receiving mentor guidance and collaborating with fellow filmmakers.

---

## Learning Experience

The program emphasizes:

- Practical filmmaking
- Story development
- Direction
- Cinematography
- Blocking
- Collaboration
- Lighting
- On-ground execution
- Mentor feedback
- Creative immersion

Theory is taught only to support practical execution.

---

## What Makes Forge Different

Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp is not:

- A passive recorded course
- A generic filmmaking workshop
- A lecture-first learning experience

Instead, it provides:

- Intensive residential learning
- Hands-on filmmaking
- Continuous mentor feedback
- Accountability
- Creative collaboration
- Practical production experience

---

## Mentorship

Participants learn from mentors and industry professionals whose work includes:

- Acclaimed projects
- Festival-level storytelling
- Mainstream platforms

The exact mentor lineup may vary by edition.

---

## Cohort Experience

Participants work inside a close-knit community of serious storytellers.

The residential environment encourages:

- Collaboration
- Creative discipline
- Peer learning
- Shared filmmaking experience

---

## Best Suited For

The bootcamp is intended for:

- Serious beginners
- Aspiring filmmakers
- Content creators transitioning into filmmaking
- Early-stage directors
- Cinematographers
- Writers
- Anyone seeking structured, immersive filmmaking practice

---

## Core Promise

Forge Filmmaking Bootcamp helps participants step into a real filmmaking environment where they:

- Learn through practice
- Build meaningful creative work
- Receive mentor guidance
- Develop practical filmmaking skills
- Grow within a focused creative community

The program aims to replace passive learning with real-world filmmaking experience through immersive, collaborative execution.
